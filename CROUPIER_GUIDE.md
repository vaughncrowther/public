# 🎩 Croupier's Guide — Casino Night Roulette

*European single-zero wheel (numbers 0–36). House edge 2.70%.*

---

## 1. Setting up

1. **Open the game** — double-click `roulette.html`; it opens in any web browser. No internet needed.
2. **Go fullscreen for the TV** — click **⛶ Fullscreen** (top-right). Best on a 32–55″ screen.
3. **Turn on sound** — tap the screen or the **🔊 Sound** button once at the start of the night. (Browsers stay silent until the first tap — after that, every spin has sound.)
4. **Best device for the French voice** — a Mac, iPhone or iPad will speak the winning number in a French accent. A plain Windows/Chromebook may use a default voice.

---

## 2. Running a round

1. **"Place your bets!"** — let the players put their chips on the table layout.
2. **Choose the spin force** (optional) — the **Gentle / Normal / Hard** buttons set how long and wild the spin is. Default is Normal.
3. **Spin the wheel** — any of these works:
   - Press the big **Spin** button, **or**
   - **Swipe** the gold bar along the bottom (a slow swipe = gentle, a fast swipe = hard), **or**
   - **Flick** the wheel itself with your finger/mouse.
4. **"Last bets!"** — a few seconds before the ball drops, a **bell dings** and the croupier voice calls *"Last bets."* No more chips after this.
5. **The result** — the ball rattles in and settles. The winning number is shown big in the centre and on the right (**Last Result**), the colour is highlighted, and the voice calls it out (e.g. *"twenty-three, red, odd"*).
6. **🎉 Zero** — if it lands on green **0**, the screen celebrates a **BIG WIN** with confetti and a fanfare.
7. **Pay & collect** — pay the winning bets (see payouts below) and clear the losing chips.
8. **History** — every result is listed down the right side. **Clear History** resets the board between sessions.

---

## 3. Payouts

Roulette payouts are quoted **"X to 1"** — you keep your original stake **and** receive X times it in winnings.
*Example: $5 straight-up win → you return the $5 stake **plus** $175 (35 × $5).*

### Inside bets (on the numbers)

| Bet | What it covers | Pays | Win chance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Straight up** | 1 number | **35 to 1** | 2.7% |
| **Split** | 2 adjacent numbers (chip on the line between) | **17 to 1** | 5.4% |
| **Street** | 3 numbers in a row | **11 to 1** | 8.1% |
| **Corner** (Square) | 4 numbers meeting at a corner | **8 to 1** | 10.8% |
| **Six Line** (Double Street) | 6 numbers (two rows) | **5 to 1** | 16.2% |

### Outside bets (around the numbers)

| Bet | What it covers | Pays | Win chance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Column** | 12 numbers (one vertical column) | **2 to 1** | 32.4% |
| **Dozen** | 12 numbers (1–12, 13–24, or 25–36) | **2 to 1** | 32.4% |
| **Red / Black** | all 18 red or all 18 black | **1 to 1** (even money) | 48.6% |
| **Odd / Even** | all 18 odd or all 18 even | **1 to 1** (even money) | 48.6% |
| **Low / High** | 1–18 or 19–36 | **1 to 1** (even money) | 48.6% |

### Quick payout ready-reckoner (per $5 chip)

| Bet | Winnings on a $5 chip |
|---|---|
| Straight up (35:1) | $175 |
| Split (17:1) | $85 |
| Street (11:1) | $55 |
| Corner (8:1) | $40 |
| Six Line (5:1) | $25 |
| Column / Dozen (2:1) | $10 |
| Red/Black, Odd/Even, Low/High (1:1) | $5 |

---

## 4. The green ZERO (0) — important

- **0 is the house number.** When the ball lands on **0**, **all** outside bets lose: Red/Black, Odd/Even, Low/High, Columns and Dozens **all lose**.
- Only chips placed **on the 0** (straight up = 35:1) or on a **split/street/corner that includes 0** win.
- This single zero is the house's 2.70% edge — keep it for the house!
